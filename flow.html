How the office works

Each founder gets his own Megazord. When Navis meet, that is a second one.

Read it as two facts. Every founder gets his own Megazord — the Navi plus the three Rangers under it. When two or more of those Navis enter the same thread, they combine again, and that is the council. The absent-human path on the right is the part that matters at 2am: the Navi decides inside the mandate, escalates outside it, and records which.
